A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Creative by design.

I’m James Lennox, an aspiring web designer and front-end developer interested in how thoughtful design and technology can make online experiences clearer and more useful.

My approach

I’m developing my understanding of the full website design process: interpreting a brief, identifying an audience, planning content, creating a visual direction, building pages and testing the result.

I value clean layouts, readable typography, consistent visual choices and websites that work well across different screen sizes. I also see feedback as an important part of improving a design rather than simply finishing the first version.

Work experience

I also bring three years of employment experience with Silverwood Industries. This experience sits alongside my current study and my developing interest in web design and front-end development.

What I’m learning

  • Structuring content with semantic HTML
  • Styling interfaces with CSS and Bootstrap
  • Applying layout, colour and typography principles
  • Testing responsive behaviour and usability
